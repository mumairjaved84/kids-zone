Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

What we collect

Almost nothing. Kids Zone has no accounts, no comments, and no newsletter — so we don't ask for your name, email, or any personal details.

Cookies

We don't set tracking cookies ourselves. Like most websites, our hosting provider (GitHub Pages) may log basic technical data such as pages visited, purely to keep the site running.

Affiliate links

Our "Check Price" and "Buy" buttons take you to AliExpress. Once you leave Kids Zone, AliExpress's own privacy policy applies to anything you do there — including any cookies they set to track referrals and pay our commission.

Children's privacy

Kids Zone is written for parents and caregivers, not for children directly, and we knowingly collect no information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.

Affiliate disclosure: Some links on this site may earn us a commission at no extra cost to you. Editorial choices remain independent.
